# My skills

Shared language for the skills in this repo.

## Language

### Slack messages

**Audience**:
The group of readers a message is written for, which fixes its structure: Team, PM or Org-wide.

**Team**:
Engineers, whether the author's own teammates or a wider engineering group channel.
_Avoid_: Group of team, teammates (as a separate audience)

**PM**:
The product manager, who reads technical details for their consequence on the product.

**Org-wide**:
Readers across the organisation who are assumed to know nothing about the project.

**When**:
In a PM message, the trigger or timeline of a technical matter.

**Side**:
In a PM message, where a technical matter happens and who is affected.
_Avoid_: Side effect

**Effect**:
In a PM message, what people notice, including unintended consequences.

**Thread reply**:
A separate follow-up draft holding technical depth that does not fit the main message.
