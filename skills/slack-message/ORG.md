# Org-wide

Readers are anyone in the organization and know nothing about the project. The usual moment is announcing something before it happens. For other moments (incident, shipped, delayed), keep the sections and adapt their content.

## Sections

```
*<Headline: what is going to happen, and when>*
*Overview* why, and the big picture
*What's changing* a few bullets
*Who's affected* who is, and what stays the same
*What you need to do* the action and deadline, or "Nothing"
*Questions* where to ask
```

The first line is the headline itself, in bold, with no "Headline" label.

## Depth and length

Expand each acronym or drop it, and explain the change by what readers will experience. About 150 words.

Also leave out sensitive internal details, such as security weaknesses, and report each removal in the notes.
