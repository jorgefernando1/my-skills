# PM

The reader is the product manager, who needs a technical matter's consequence on the product, often to decide something or set expectations. For other moments (incident, shipped, delayed), keep the sections and adapt their content.

## Sections

```
*Summary* the product consequence in 1–2 lines
*When* the trigger or timeline: when it happens, or when it lands
*Side* where it happens (frontend, backend, third party) and which users or customers are affected
*Effect* what people notice and what it means for the product, including unintended consequences
*What I need from you* the decision, with options and my recommendation, or "FYI only"
```

## Depth and length

Technical detail appears only as far as it explains the effect, in plain words: describe behavior instead of showing code or identifiers. About 150 words.
