# Team

Readers are engineers: the user's teammates or a wider engineering channel. The usual moment is explaining something hard the user found, built or decided, such as a root cause, a design choice or a migration. For other moments (incident, shipped, delayed), keep the sections and adapt their content.

## Sections

```
*TL;DR* the conclusion in 1–2 lines
*Context* why this came up
*What I found / decided* the technical explanation
*Why this way* alternatives and trade-offs
*What it means for you* actions, risks, review or input needed
```

Use "we" in a label when the work was shared.

## Depth and length

Use real identifiers, error text and short snippets. About 250 words.
